# Gameco Video Game Analysis （2006-2016）

## 1. Project Background & Business Objectives
As a data analyst at **GameCo**,the objective of this project is to perform a comprehensive descriptive and diagnostic market analysis using historical global video game sales data (spanning from 2006 to 2016).

The insights derived from this analysis are designed to inform executive decision-making for the upcoming 2017 budget planning and marketing strategies. Key business questions addressed include:
* **Regional Sales Trends**: How do sales figures vary across major geographic regions (North America, Europe, and Japan) over time?
* **Genre Preferences**: Are certain game genres significantly more popular in specific regional markets?
* **Competitor Analysis**: Who are the primary publishing competitors dominating each market?
* **Strategic Budget Allocation**: How can GameCo optimize marketing spend and product development focus based on historical performance?

---

## 2. Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Processing & Analysis**: Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Data Filtering, Descriptive Statistics)
* **Analytical Frameworks**: Descriptive & Diagnostic Analysis, Multivariate Analysis, Hypothesis Formulation

## 3. Key Findings & Insights

### Regional Sales Performance & Trends
* **North America (NA)**: Experienced a major sales **peak around 2008**, followed by a sharp **decline post-2010**. **Shooter games** dominate the market, accounting for **56%** of total sales. Key competitor: **Activision**.
* **Europe (EU)**: Reached **peak sales in 2009** with a more gradual decline thereafter. Notably, **Racing games** are the most popular genre, representing **33%** of sales. Key competitor: **Electronic Arts (EA)**.
* **Japan (JP)**: Demonstrated a relatively stable yet steadily shrinking market through 2016。 The market shows an overwhelming preference for Role-Playing Games (RPGs), representing **38%** of total sales. Key competitor: **Nintendo**.

### Evolution of Product Popularity Over Time
* Historical analysis indicates that the popularity of legacy franchise games and specific hardware titles (e.g., Wii games, Super Mario, Grand Theft Auto, Tetris, Call of Duty) has shifted over time, emphasizing the need for continuous market monitoring.

---

## 4. Strategic Recommendations for GameCo

Based on the empirical findings, the following actionable recommendations were presented to GameCo executives:
1. **Targeted Regional Investment**:
   * **North America**: Deprioritize underperforming genres (like RPGs and Strategy) and reallocate budgets toward **Shooter, Platform, and Sports** games.
   * **Europe**: Focus investments on **Racing** games while deprioritizing RPGs, puzzles, and fighting genres.
   * **Japan**: Adopt a niche strategy focused on localized **RPG development** while carefully managing capital investment due to market contraction.
2. **Business Model Innovation**:
   * Develop an online sales platform (similar to Amazon) to capture broader market segments.
   * Pivot toward mobile and digital gaming ecosystems in partnership with industry leaders like Nintendo, Namco Bandai, or Electronic Arts.
